Favorite CD Releases & Reviews

Hummin' Comin' at 'Cha (1993)
This debut album introduced Xscape’s powerful harmonies and raw 90s R&B sound. It set the tone for their signature style.
Off the Hook (1995)
A smoother, more polished project that shows their vocal growth. The production feels richer, and the harmonies are tighter.
Traces of My Lipstick (1998)
A mature, emotional album with standout ballads. Their voices blend beautifully, making this one of their strongest releases.
