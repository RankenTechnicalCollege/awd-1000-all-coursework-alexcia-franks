My Favorite Recipe: Red Beans and Rice

Ingredients

  • 1 lb red beans
  • 1 lb smoked sausage (sliced)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt, pepper, and Cajun seasoning
  • 6 cups water or broth
  • Cooked white rice

Steps

  1. Rinse the red beans and soak them overnight or for at least 4 hours.
  2. Sauté the onion, bell pepper, celery, and garlic until softened.
  3. Add sliced sausage and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add beans and water/broth, then season with salt, pepper, and Cajun seasoning.
  5. Simmer for 1.5–2 hours until beans are tender and creamy.
  6. Serve over cooked white rice.
