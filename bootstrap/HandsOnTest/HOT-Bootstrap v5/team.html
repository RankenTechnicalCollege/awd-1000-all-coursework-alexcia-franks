Meet Our Team

Alexcia Franks

Alexcia Franks is the founder and owner of Fresh Start Cleaning Services. With more than ten years of experience in residential cleaning, she is committed to helping families maintain clean, healthy, and welcoming homes.

Alexcia oversees daily operations, trains new employees, and ensures that every customer receives exceptional service. Her leadership and attention to detail have helped Fresh Start Cleaning Services build a reputation for reliability and customer satisfaction.

Michael Davis

Michael Davis serves as the Lead Cleaning Specialist for Fresh Start Cleaning Services. He coordinates cleaning assignments and helps ensure that each home receives thorough and professional care.

Michael is known for his strong work ethic and commitment to quality. He enjoys working directly with customers and takes pride in delivering results that exceed expectations while maintaining the highest standards of cleanliness.
